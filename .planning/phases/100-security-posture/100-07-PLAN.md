<objective>
Close criterion 4. Both tables have RLS enabled and ZERO policies, which denies every non-superuser
role - but they are not the same problem and one template applied twice would hide that.

`public.intelligence_email_queue` grants every privilege to `anon` and `authenticated` while having no
policy: grants those roles can never exercise. Its only writer is
`backend/src/adapters/intelligence/smtp-adapter.ts:16` via `supabaseAdmin`. Intent: service-role only.

`events.idempotency_keys` grants them nothing at all. Intent: service-role only, already enforced by
grants; what it lacks is the written statement.

**The policy assertion includes its EXPRESSIONS.** A policy named `p100_service_role_only` for ALL to
`service_role` with `USING (false)` enforces the opposite of its name, and a row that showed only
name/command/roles would grade it green. `polqual` and `polwithcheck` are rendered and required to be
`true`/`true`, the mode must be PERMISSIVE, and **exactly two** policies may exist across the pair.
</objective>
