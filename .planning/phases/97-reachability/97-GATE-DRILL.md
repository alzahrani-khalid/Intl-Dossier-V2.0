# 97-GATE-DRILL — every gate in Phase 97, examined once, by one author, in one pass

**Written by plan `97-12` (Wave 5), 2026-08-17. Single writer of this file.**
**Subject set:** the 29 automated-gate blocks in the twelve `97-NN-PLAN.md` files, as they stand
now.

`GATE-STANDARD-P92.md`'s pass procedure exists because **isolation is the mechanism**: in Phase 92
every repair was locally correct and the SET was wrong. So all twelve plans are held here at once,
by one seat, rather than each plan grading itself. The per-plan observations were made by their own
executors; this record consolidates them, attributes each, and adds the cross-plan checks no single
plan can perform.

Gates are named `plan.taskN` throughout — never by line number, and never by their opening tag
literal. **Both are deliberate.** Line numbers shift the moment any earlier clause is deleted, and
this leg produced exactly that ambiguity (the same two gates were called `:248`/`:326` and `g1`/`g2`
in different messages, and after a clause deletion neither number matched). The tag literal is
avoided because `gate-drill.mjs` pairs an opening tag written in PROSE with the next real closing
tag and extracts the text between as a spurious gate.

## THE VERDICT VOCABULARY, AND THE RULE THAT DECIDES THE AMBIGUOUS CASE

`GATE-STANDARD-P92.md` fixes exactly three verdicts:

| verdict                           | meaning                                                                                                                    |
| --------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `SOUND`                           | red observed on the undone tree, green observed on a fully constructed done state, no C2–C10 exception outstanding         |
| `REPAIRED (what changed)`         | as above, but the gate or its consumed artifact was edited under a ruling; an edited gate is a NEW gate and was re-drilled |
| `CANNOT CONSTRUCT (what and why)` | no green direction was observed on a fully constructed done state                                                          |

**THERE IS NO FOURTH TERM FOR "HALF CONSTRUCTED", AND THE STANDARD ALREADY ANSWERS IT: a gate whose
done state was not FULLY constructed is `CANNOT CONSTRUCT`, never `SOUND`.** That rule is stated
here, at the top, so no row below splits the difference. Where a row is `CANNOT CONSTRUCT` it names
which half was constructed and which was not.

**The set is otherwise complete — said explicitly rather than left implicit.** Every one of the 29
rows carries one of the three terms; no row invents a term; no row is absent.

### The plan predicted six half-constructed gates. At execution there are ZERO, and here is the derivation

partial-construction rule above therefore governs **no row**. Two of those six are still
`CANNOT CONSTRUCT` — but for the opposite reason: their behavioural half RAN and could not reach its
threshold because of a defect outside their subject, which is a measured ceiling, not an
unconstructed half.

### The two things the whole-set drill established that a per-gate drill cannot

1. **A gate is only fully observed at the moment it FIRST GOES GREEN, and nobody is looking then.**
